# Scene assets and authorship

## Penelope / complete seated figure

- Original scan: Three D Scans, initiated by Oliver Laric. Work page: https://threedscans.com/vienna/penelope/
- Real-time mesh, UVs, normal map and ambient-occlusion map: Keijiro, ThreeDScans repository: https://github.com/keijiro/ThreeDScans/tree/master/Assets/ThreeDScans/Penelope
- Repository's original reuse statement: https://github.com/keijiro/ThreeDScans/blob/master/Assets/ThreeDScans/Acknowledgement.txt
- The accompanying statement says the scanned models are not copyrighted and may be used without restriction. This preview preserves that statement; it does not relabel the scan as CC0.
- Local original statement: `SOURCE-ACKNOWLEDGEMENT.txt`.
- Changes: normalized world coordinates and scale; one smoothing subdivision; ivory material; 2K normal and ambient-occlusion textures; modern headphones added as separate geometry. The full scan and its integral stone seat remain intact.
- The sculpture is external cultural-heritage geometry, not Ryan Wang's artwork. Its thoughtful seated pose differs from the generated concept's typing pose.
